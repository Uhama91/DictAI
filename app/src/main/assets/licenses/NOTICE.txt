DictAI
======

DictAI is a modified derivative of Phone Whisper by kafkasl.

Upstream provenance
-------------------
The imported upstream snapshot is commit 8356089931a6f771facc18796c8912460589ec2b
from https://github.com/kafkasl/phone-whisper. The corresponding upstream
Apache-2.0 license was added upstream in commit
e6518cda437ed2c266f36cf476db857fa2690d87. The upstream snapshot used for this
fork did not contain a NOTICE file.

Files derived from Phone Whisper and modified for DictAI carry a short
modification notice. New files and third-party components may have separate
terms; see app/src/main/assets/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt and
app/src/main/assets/licenses/.
